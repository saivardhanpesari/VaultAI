Frequently Asked Questions

How secure is VaultAI? +

VaultAI is built with strict multi-tenancy and data isolation. Your documents are stored securely and never used to train generalized models without your consent. (Backend implementation pending).

What file types are supported? +

We support standard business formats including PDF, DOCX, and TXT files for knowledge extraction and querying.

Do you hallucinate answers? +

Our system uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ground answers in your uploaded documents and provides clear source citations so you can verify the information.
